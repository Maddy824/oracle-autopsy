**Build a daily decision brief around “what changed my next action?”** Put open positions, exits, earnings candidates and data quality first. News belongs where it explains a relevant event; most market commentary belongs in a small context strip.

**P1 — How discretionary Indian traders actually decide**

Published processes suggest a sequence: narrow the universe, identify a setup, define invalidation, size the position, then wait. They differ substantially in what constitutes a setup.

- **Vijay Thakkar:** His December 2022 interview describes weekly scanning for 52-week highs, inspecting weekly/monthly charts, identifying old resistance, and waiting for a breakout with volume. He specifies entry proximity, a stop and staged exits. Beginners are advised to allocate 10% of capital per stock. Crucially, the article’s approximately 8% “risk per trade” describes the stock’s entry-to-stop distance; at 10% allocation, that is approximately 0.8% of account equity before gaps and costs. His return claims are **UNVERIFIED**, self-reported performance. [Moneycontrol interview](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/traders-edge-a-trading-strategy-sans-technical-indicators-for-creating-wealth-9759301.html)
- **Sudarshan Sukhani:** A dated August 2025 sample newsletter makes the decision state explicit: buy tomorrow, buy above a trigger, hold, or exit. It records entry dates, stops and rationales; one stock framework specifies 15-day holding and closing-basis stops. This is valuable evidence of how a trading plan is communicated, not proof that its recommendations outperform. [Technical Trends sample](https://www.technicaltrends.in/downloads/Sample.pdf)
- **Karthik Rangappa/Varsity:** The published workflow filters a small shortlist through price structure, volume, support/resistance and reward-to-risk. Sometimes nothing qualifies. Its useful contribution is sequential rejection and a predefined exit—not importing every indicator into this project. [Varsity’s process](https://zerodha.com/varsity/chapter/finale-helping-get-started/?comments=all)

A more explicit contemporary routine comes from **Rohit Musale**, June 2026: morning screening, reject extended stocks and unsuitable trends, mark buying/stopping ranges, then use alerts. He sells training, and effectiveness is **UNVERIFIED**. Nevertheless, it demonstrates that a discretionary trader can work from a bounded checklist without continuous financial news. [Published routine](https://rohitmusale.substack.com/p/the-daily-trading-routine-of-a-professional)

**Application:** Weekly review maintains the universe and examines mistakes. Evening work prepares tomorrow’s decisions. Morning work checks changes and executable conditions. Published sources do not establish one universal professional timetable; that schedule is my synthesis. Keep the owner’s existing sizing rule rather than mixing practitioners’ percentages.

**P2 — What an institutional morning note contains**

Public institutional publications show the information architecture, although they cannot reveal confidential desk discussions.

HDFC Securities’ *Morning Cuppa* example combines previous-session performance, a timestamped GIFT Nifty reading, institutional flows and company developments. The retrieved example contains July 2023 observations: useful as a template, unsuitable as current data. Aditya Birla Sun Life’s December 2025 market pulse includes sector/style performance, derivatives statistics, futures basis, rollover, both volume and OI put–call ratios, global indices and commodities. [HDFC example](https://www.hdfcsec.com/morning-cuppa), [ABSL market pulse](https://mutualfund.adityabirlacapital.com/-/media/knowledgecenter/pdf/all-about-market/daily-equity-market-pulse---december-30-2025.pdf)

For India, the fuller desk checklist comprises:

- Overnight US/Asian markets, GIFT Nifty, crude, USD/INR and bond yields.
- FII/DII provisional cash flows; futures/options positioning, PCR, rollover and India VIX.
- Results, board meetings, corporate actions, bulk/block transactions and company news.
- RBI decisions, Indian CPI/IIP, US Fed/CPI and other scheduled releases.

**Their purposes differ.** GIFT Nifty helps assess opening conditions; yields, currency and crude identify economic exposures; calendars identify event risk; positioning describes outstanding commitments.

These are not interchangeable bullish/bearish votes. Every open derivative contract has opposing sides; OI alone does not identify informed conviction. Rollover can represent hedges and spreads.

For this owner, a macro item becomes operationally relevant when linked to a holding or execution risk. “Crude moved” is context. “This holding has an identified crude-cost exposure; a new announcement needs review” is a task. Neither establishes a profitable trading rule.

**P3 — What is measurable and predictively supported at 1–20 days**

The evidence is narrower than the list of available indicators.

| Input | Evidence and practical interpretation |
|---|---|
| Earnings information and subsequent price reaction | Relevant research exists, but earnings growth, consensus surprise and positive market reaction are different variables. Kundu–Banerjee’s 2021 Indian study examines 5–25-day windows and finds substantial pre-announcement pricing and asymmetric subsequent adjustment. It does not validate the project’s exact entry rule. [Paper](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/23322039.2021.1898112) |
| India VIX | Indian research tests 1-, 5-, 10- and 20-day volatility forecasts, including out-of-sample comparisons, and finds value from incorporating implied volatility. This supports forecasting **movement/risk**, not whether tomorrow rises. [Narwal, Sheera and Mittal, 2016](https://www.journalpressindia.com/delhi-business-review/doi/10.51768/dbr.v17i2.172201606) |
| PCR | A 2019 Indian study reports horizon-dependent predictive relationships, using 2001–2013 data. That prevents dismissing PCR categorically as noise. Frequency-domain causality, however, is not an executable, contemporary, after-cost strategy. Contract and expiry structures have also changed. [Study](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7099/7/1/24) |
| Volume surprise, sector strength, breadth, price location | Straightforward to calculate prospectively. Incremental value beyond the chosen earnings rule remains **UNVERIFIED** for this universe, horizon and cost structure. Archive as research candidates. |
| Flows, rollover, delivery percentage, headline sentiment | No sufficiently convincing contemporary, point-in-time, after-cost evidence was established here to promote them into entry rules. “Unproven” is more accurate than “always useless.” |

Two traps deserve emphasis. **A variable measured daily need not predict daily returns.** Monthly momentum evidence does not automatically validate a five-day trade. And event studies may use the announcement date without proving that a trader could obtain the relevant information before the assumed fill.

NSE explicitly labels FII/DII data provisional and revisable after custodial confirmation. Preserve that initial observation; never replace it silently with a later number. Depository FPI series also require matching segment, scope and date definitions before comparison. [NSE methodology](https://www.nseindia.com/reports/fii-dii?os=apprefdapp)

**P4 — Risk, behaviour and no-trade decisions**

The existing note already contains SEBI’s loss statistics. The additional lesson is methodological: SEBI’s association between frequent trading and losses does **not** prove that reducing any particular trader’s frequency creates an edge. It does justify treating unnecessary turnover as a measurable failure mode. [SEBI study, July 2024](https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/research/jul-2024/study-analysis-of-intraday-trading-by-individuals-in-equity-cash-segment_84946.html)

Lo, Repin and Steenbarger’s study of 80 day traders associated stronger emotional reactions to gains and losses with worse performance. It was observational and outside India; it does not establish a specific cooling-off prescription. [Study, 2005](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=690501)

I found no strong India-specific causal evidence that journaling alone improves net returns. Its defensible purpose is **auditability**: distinguish a losing valid trade from a rule violation, data error or execution problem.

Precommit to entry, sizing and exit before seeing the next price move. Record urges to recover losses or increase size after wins. A no-trade day should have a reason: no qualifying event, insufficient capital, failed regime condition, stale data, or inability to execute safely. It should not count as a failed day.

**P5 — News and event data engineering**

The important distinction is **publicly visible versus licensed for automated collection and retention**.

**Exchange announcements and calendars.** NSE’s official RSS catalogue includes announcements, financial results, board meetings, corporate actions, insider trading and relevant ownership disclosures. It explicitly supports feed subscriptions and acknowledges possible reader/provider delays. No measured end-to-end latency guarantee was established. [NSE RSS](https://www.nseindia.com/static/rss-feed)

However, NSE’s general terms prohibit systematic automated website collection and impose restrictions on storage and reuse, including simulation uses. A supported RSS subscription is not blanket permission to scrape undocumented JSON endpoints or build a permanent backtest database. Automated archival and research rights need explicit verification. [NSE terms](https://www.nseindia.com/static/nse-terms-of-use)

Use the official [announcement](https://www.nseindia.com/companies-listing/corporate-filings-announcements) and [board-meeting](https://www.nseindia.com/companies-listing/corporate-filings-board-meetings) pages for verification and offered downloads. Historical search availability does not establish complete historical publication vintages. A scheduled results meeting is not proof that results have been released.

**BSE:** The RSS pages attempted were inaccessible in this research session; current endpoint reliability, latency and archival permission remain **UNVERIFIED**. SEBI’s June 2025 order documents historical dissemination problems. It does not establish a fixed delay today. Avoid repeating claims such as “BSE RSS is always five minutes late.” [SEBI order](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2025/order_bse_matter.pdf)

**Deals, ownership and market reports.**

- NSE provides historical bulk/block reports and explicitly says records can change following member requests. Save the version observed; trade date is not publication time. [Deals archive](https://www.nseindia.com/report-detail/display-bulk-and-block-deals)
- PIT/SAST disclosures describe earlier transactions or ownership changes. Use disclosure availability, not acquisition date, as the earliest signal time; distinguish purchases from transfers and encumbrance changes.
- F&O ban files specify the next trading date. They concern derivatives; a ban is not automatically a prohibition on cash delivery purchases. [NSE ban report](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-risk-management-sec-ban)
- India VIX has historical values and represents expected volatility over 30 calendar days. FII/DII reports are provisional. Exact daily publication deadlines remain **UNVERIFIED**; detect arrival rather than assuming a clock time. [VIX definition/history](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/indices-indiavix-index)

For prices, use the owner’s authorised Zerodha data access. For exchange files, use permitted downloads/imports until automated collection and retention rights are established.

**Official macro sources.** RBI describes RSS for press releases and notifications, although the main RSS page was inaccessible here. Its historical official help page confirms the intended subscription mechanism. [RBI RSS guidance](https://systemhealth.rbi.org.in/Scripts/rss.aspx%281%29.html)

MoSPI’s calendar is public, but its dynamic calendar did not render reliably. Actual dated release notices matter: a June 2026 CPI release specifies **16:00**, whereas an older FAQ still says 17:30. Preserve release schedules, base years and provisional/final vintages. [MoSPI calendar](https://mospi.gov.in/advance-release-calendar), [dated CPI release](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1781259853555_04875571-19f2-4830-9cad-3a1bfd5992e1_Press_Release_of_CPI_for_May_2026.pdf)

For US events, BLS offers a subscribable calendar; the Fed publishes meeting dates and linked historical statements. Convert `America/New_York` to IST with daylight-saving rules. US Treasury offers a documented daily XML yield feed—daily observations, not live quotes. [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/empsit.htm?categoryId=1&orient=1), [Fed](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm), [Treasury XML](https://home.treasury.gov/treasury-daily-interest-rate-xml-feed)

**P6 — The skeptical case for less information**

There is direct experimental evidence against equating information quantity with quality. A *Review of Accounting Studies* experiment held predictive quality constant: more information increased confidence and trading aggressiveness, while greater quantity reduced expected wealth in the laboratory market. This is stronger than an influencer’s claim, although not an Indian field trial. [Study, published 2009](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11142-009-9106-7)

Barber and Odean also find individuals disproportionately buy attention-grabbing stocks, including stocks in the news and with extreme returns or volume. A news-heavy interface can therefore change which stocks attract consideration before any disciplined evaluation. [*All That Glitters*, 2008](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers%20current%20versions/allthatglitters_rfs_2008.pdf)

**Design implication:** Cap the brief by decisions, not available headlines. Display changes since the previous edition. Keep optional context collapsed. Evaluate success by missed obligations, rule violations, costs and execution quality—not reading time or trade count.

**Synthesis — the one-page daily brief**

Use **18:30 IST** as an evening publication target, conditional on official data availability, followed by an **08:45 IST changes-only refresh**. These are proposed operating times, not claims about exchange release guarantees. Respect holidays and special sessions.

| Section | Contents and source | Timing | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Status** | Strategy version; paper/live eligibility; portfolio reconciliation; price/filing freshness; missing jobs. Local ledger and authorised broker data. | Both | **Decision input** |
| **Open positions first** | Each holding: quantity, entry, holding-session count, closing-stop condition, scheduled exit, new filing, upcoming corporate action. Broker/ledger plus exchange filings. | Evening; overnight changes at 08:45 | **Decision input** |
| **Earnings candidates** | Filing link/time; completed reaction session; stock and Nifty returns; existing regime test; rank; proposed whole-share size, costs and exit rule. | Evening | **Decision input under existing rules** |
| **Event exceptions** | Relevant results revisions, suspensions, corporate actions and operational restrictions affecting holdings/candidates. Official notices. | Both | **Review/operational input** |
| **Next 48 hours** | Relevant company events; RBI, Indian CPI/IIP, Fed/US CPI; scheduled time and timezone. Official calendars. | Both | **Risk context** unless a rule is preregistered |
| **Market strip** | Nifty regime status; VIX; timestamped GIFT/global, crude, currency/yield observations from permitted sources. Optional provisional flows. | Evening; refresh available observations at 08:45 | Only the existing Nifty rule is a signal; remainder **context** |
| **Decision** | Planned exits, eligible entries, deferred reviews, or “no new trade”; brief reason and owner acknowledgement. | Both | **Action record** |

Keep at most three relevant news summaries. An empty news section is acceptable.

For each holding, end with a plain instruction such as “scheduled exit next session” or “no rule-triggered change.” If holdings are unavailable, show **PORTFOLIO UNRECONCILED** rather than implying zero exposure.

Two timing safeguards are essential:

1. Results published overnight have not completed their first full reaction session at 08:45. They enter the event queue, not tomorrow’s already-qualified entry list.
2. Recheck executable price and availability around the planned 09:20 entry. Any new gap/slippage veto changes implementation and must be specified and tested; it cannot be improvised from morning sentiment.

Also preserve the strategy’s closing-stop semantics. Replacing that with an intraday broker stop would change the strategy.

**Pre-trade checklist and journal**

Before an entry:

1. Is this strategy authorised for the current paper/live stage?
2. Are portfolio, cash, prices and filings reconciled and fresh?
3. Has the required full reaction session completed?
4. Do the existing eligibility, ranking and regime rules pass?
5. Does whole-share sizing satisfy risk, allocation and position limits, including estimated costs?
6. Are exit timing and closing-stop instructions recorded?
7. Is this a planned trade, without an urge to recover losses or chase news?

A compact journal row:

> Decision timestamp · brief/strategy version · symbol/event link · evidence IDs · enter/hold/exit/skip and reason · intended quantity/price/stop/exit date · estimated costs · actual fill/time/fees · rule deviation · emotional state · outcome · one review lesson.

Record rejected candidates and no-trade days. Review weekly; score adherence separately from profit.

**What to archive for future factor tests**

Keep the current strategy frozen. Later hypotheses could test reaction magnitude, abnormal turnover, closing location, overnight gap, sector-relative reaction, VIX regime, announcement timing, competing announcement count, and structured earnings/guidance changes. Flows and PCR belong in a lower-priority research queue.

For each observation, retain—where licensed:

- Source URL, immutable document/feed version and checksum.
- Event time, stated publication time, first-seen time, ingestion time and feature-ready time.
- Original timezone, reporting period, units, revision status and source permissions.
- Point-in-time universe membership, identifiers, raw prices and corporate-action records.
- Parser/model/prompt versions, extracted evidence and the actual brief delivered.

Use append-only revisions and explicit “supersedes” links. A replay may use a feature only after its required data and computation were available. Backfilled records must not appear as though captured live.

Store missing-source intervals and deduplicate NSE/BSE copies without losing their separate timestamps. Archive calendars before events occur. Retain contemporaneous LLM outputs; rerunning next year’s model over old news does not recreate today’s knowledge.

After a year, test a small preregistered set with realistic costs and earnings-cluster-aware validation. One year may still contain too few independent events.

**What not to include**

Exclude generic stock tips, influencer P&L, daily profit targets, “smart money” narratives inferred from OI, ungrounded sentiment scores, duplicate headlines, and compulsory trades. Keep broad block-deal, insider and F&O tables off the main page unless relevant to a holding or candidate.

Most importantly, do not manufacture a market-direction score by averaging unrelated indicators. The brief should make an existing decision clearer—and make “nothing to do today” easy to accept.
