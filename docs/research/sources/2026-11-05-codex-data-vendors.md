**Oracle Autopsy — NSE data vendor research**  
**Checked: 5 November 2026.** No repository files were edited.

My recommendation is **Kite Connect for longer intraday history, Tick Data for the strongest documented corporate-action and inactive-stock coverage, and Upstox for the cheapest practical starting point**. Corporate announcements and bulk/block deals will probably require a separate source.

All prices below were observed on the date above. **UNVERIFIED** means the public documentation did not establish the claim; it does not necessarily mean the feature is unavailable. This was documentation research, without authenticated downloads or sample-data validation.

**3. Top three recommendations**

1. **Best value for multi-year 5-minute history: Zerodha Kite Connect — ₹500/month.**

   Its approximately 2015-onward minute archive offers materially more development history than Upstox, including pre-2020 market conditions. Native 5-minute data avoids downloading every 1-minute bar merely to aggregate it. Dhan and FYERS Prime are similarly priced alternatives, but their current published intraday entitlement is around five years or more, with less clearly established earliest coverage. [Kite coverage](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/14149/historical-data-retention-policy), [price](https://zerodha.com/products/api/).

   **Conditions:** validate the intended stock universe, reconstruct raw prices where necessary, source dividends separately, and clarify private paper-replay licensing. Kite is a strong low-cost history source, not a verified complete inactive-stock database.

2. **Best documented basis for corporate-action-correct daily data: Tick Data — premium, quote-dependent.**

   It provides the strongest documented combination here: as-traded records, corporate actions including dividends/spin-offs, symbol mapping and inactive companies. Daily bars can be derived consistently from its intraday records. That fits Oracle Autopsy’s separation of execution prices and adjusted signals better than an adjusted-only feed. This is a recommendation based on documented capabilities, **not a completed quality audit**. [NSE dataset](https://www.tickdata.com/equity-data/national-stock-exchange-of-india).

   Its lease and minimum commitment may be excessive for an individual project. Obtain an **EOD-only/bulk quotation from Accelpix or GDFL** as a cheaper comparison, requiring raw prices, action records and the adjustment formula explicitly. Their all-in cost and equivalent historical completeness remain **UNVERIFIED**.

   I would **not recommend EODHD for NSE daily data** based on this research: the current exchange catalogue does not establish NSE support.

3. **Cheapest practical path: retain Upstox, supplemented by official NSE records — ₹0 incremental API subscription.**

   January 2022 onward is enough to begin a roughly four-year research programme while reserving twelve months, depending on the chosen cutoff. Upstox now documents a corporate-actions endpoint, potentially reducing manual adjustment work. Test its entitlement and historical completeness before relying on it. [History](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-historical-candle-data/), [corporate actions](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-corporate-actions/).

   **Angel One is worth a free comparison trial** if older history is essential and another broker account is acceptable. Its advertised archive is deeper, but symbol-level completeness and adjustment quality need checking. Staying with Upstox has the lowest integration cost.

**4. Announcements, filings and bulk/block deals**

**GDFL is the clearest paid candidate for this requirement:** its corporate-data catalogue explicitly includes historical announcements, corporate actions, annual reports and bulk/block deals. Pricing, earliest event dates, original attachments and exact dissemination-timestamp fields remain **UNVERIFIED**. [Corporate-data catalogue](https://docs.globaldatafeeds.in/fundamental-data-api-introduction-923681m0).

**TrueData and Accelpix** both document announcement and corporate-action services. Their public pages did not establish complete multi-year bulk/block-deal coverage or the timestamp guarantees your event strategies need. [TrueData](https://www.truedata.in/products/marketdataapi), [Accelpix](https://accelpix.com/products/pix-api/).

**Official NSE corporate data is expensive:** EOD corporate announcements cost **₹5,00,000/year**, delivered after **20:00 IST through SFTP**; the corporate feed is **₹10,60,000/year**, with dedicated connectivity requirements. These are separate from price-history products. [NSE corporate subscription](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/corporate-data-subscription).

For Oracle Autopsy, an event’s trade date or ex-date is insufficient. Require the original document, exchange dissemination time, revisions and an identifier mapping. Without those, a bulk-deal or results strategy can accidentally trade before the information was public.

**5. Project-specific implications before buying**

- **Upstox downloads are not raw execution prices.** Its October 2026 clarification says historical candles are split/bonus-adjusted only, with no raw-series option or published adjustment factors. Your README’s `with_actions(raw, actions)` workflow therefore needs genuine raw prices or a validated reconstruction; otherwise share multipliers can double-count an adjustment. [Upstox clarification](https://community.upstox.com/t/clarification-on-upstox-historical-candle-v3-data-usage-for-personal-backtesting-and-ml-research/18061).
- **Dividend handling remains separate.** The README documents split/bonus share multipliers, not dividend cash flows. Buying dividend-adjusted prices alone does not make the execution ledger dividend-correct.
- **Inactive stocks and historical index membership are different requirements.** Even an archive containing delisted companies does not automatically provide point-in-time Nifty 50/100 constituents. Using today’s constituents throughout history still introduces survivorship/selection bias.
- **Nifty spot is not Nifty futures or an ETF.** Request the spot index explicitly. Index “volume” may be zero or vendor-derived; Accelpix, for example, describes constituent-aggregated index volume. Your investable buy-and-hold benchmark still needs actual ETF prices. [Accelpix field definitions](https://accelpix.com/products/pix-api/).
- **NSE snapshots are not automatically interval OHLCV.** Published CM snapshot tariffs are **₹16 lakh/year for 1-minute**, **₹4.2 lakh/year for 5-minute**, and **₹1.2 lakh/year for 15-minute-delayed 1-minute snapshots**. Do not purchase these assuming they are a historical candle archive. [Official tariff](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/mediaattachment/2026-04/Download_Pricing_file_-_Domestic_clients_20260424122229.pdf).
- **Freeze the complete research input.** Keep downloaded prices, action tables, instrument mappings and event records together with hashes. Later vendor corrections must create a new dataset version, rather than silently altering the sealed year.
- **Audit samples before a large purchase.** Check a quiet session, a volatile session, a split/bonus date, a dividend date, a renamed/merged stock and an inactive stock. Reconcile daily totals against NSE records and distinguish missing bars from genuine no-trade intervals. No vendor’s gap rate was independently measured here.
